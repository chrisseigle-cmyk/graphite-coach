Turn a portrait photo into a pencil study, then draw it yourself with a fixed ten-step plan.

    Slide right for more, smaller cells. Draw the same grid on your paper so every square matches.

    The coaching is a fixed library of steps written ahead of time. Your photo only changes the overlay images and which pencil each step names, so the same photo always gives the same plan. Your photo never leaves your device. In the iPhone version, face landmarks will add proportion guides (eye line, nose and mouth thirds) to step 3. Lines are drawn by the Informative Drawings model (Chan, Durand & Isola, 2022) and the background is removed by U²-Net (Qin et al., 2020), both running on your device. Sketches you start are saved in this browser on this device only.
