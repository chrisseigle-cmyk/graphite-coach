About and privacy Your photo never leaves your device

The coaching is a fixed library of steps written ahead of time. Your photo only changes the overlay images and which pencil each step names, so the same photo always gives the same plan. Your photo never leaves your device. Lines are drawn by the Informative Drawings model (Chan, Durand & Isola, 2022), the subject is found by U²-Net (Qin et al., 2020) and facial features by Google's MediaPipe Face Landmarker (Apache-2.0), all running on your device. Sketches you start are saved in this browser on this device only.
