Graphite Coach

Turn a portrait photo into a pencil study, then draw it yourself with a fixed seven-step plan.

Sample: plaster head study (upload your own photo)

Harder leads cap how dark the drawing can go. Softer leads reach deeper blacks with more grain.

    The coaching is a fixed library of steps written ahead of time. Your photo only changes the overlay images and which pencil each step names, so the same photo always gives the same plan. Your photo never leaves your device. In the iPhone version, face landmarks will add proportion guides (eye line, nose and mouth thirds) to step 2.
